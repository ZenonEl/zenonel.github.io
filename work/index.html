Commercial work

Some of the work behind my CV.

I work on backends, product integrations and applied AI features. Requirements usually reach me through management; I turn them into implementation and check the result.

These cases cover payment paths through to their real effects, an import that needed a rollback, and metrics that were measuring the wrong thing. Each page follows the work far enough to explain the decision.

Client names and private project material are omitted.
