About

01The shape of my work

Most of my commercial work is backend in Python: product APIs, payment and document flows, data pipelines, and applied AI features. I have also worked commercially with PHP. My C# experience comes from personal projects; my public repositories also include Rust and TypeScript.

I am useful early, when requirements still arrive as conversation. I write down the flows, find the missing decisions, and agree on what can actually be checked before I settle on the implementation.

02How I use AI agents

I use agents to read unfamiliar code, compare options, and draft bounded changes. They save a lot of time, but their answers still need checking.

Agents inspect and implement; I decide what the requirement means, where the boundary goes, and what counts as verified. I do not read the code line by line, so the checking is split. Subagent reviewers look at how the code is built, round after round until LGTM. I check how it behaves: by hand and in live runs against the scenarios in the requirements, and then I confirm that what was asked for is there and nothing got lost on the way. Each catches what the other misses: on 28 May the tests did not notice that a graph had silently stopped saving results, and a live run showed it. More in the collection on the working loop.

On money paths and anything irreversible a change does not reach production until its retry and failure branches have been checked live.

03How I keep myself honest

I measure before changing a system when the result can be measured. I also try a deliberately wrong input: if the API, test, or counter accepts that too, the first green result did not prove much.

Every change goes through review. When review finds something, a large or risky fix gets another round and a small one usually does not. My reports separate what I checked from what I did not. The examples, including several times this caught my own mistake, are collected in When a passing check misses the problem.

04How I write about the work

A result on its own says little about the engineering behind it. In the cases I keep the decision, the option I rejected, the way I checked the result, and the limit of that check.

Client details stay private, but the mechanism and my part in the work should remain concrete enough to discuss in an interview.

05What I am looking for

Backend and applied AI work where the problem is not yet well posed and the result has to be dependable. Remote; an office only in my own city.

The site is available in English and Russian. GitHub is my public contact route and the best place to see what I am building now.
