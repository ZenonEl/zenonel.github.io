About

About

Backend engineer, mostly Python, about two years of commercial work. I like problems where the requirement is still unclear and somebody has to make it concrete.

Last reviewed 2026-09-07

01The shape of my work

Most of my commercial work is backend in Python: product APIs, payment and document flows, data pipelines, and applied AI features. I have also worked commercially with PHP. My C# experience comes from personal projects; my public repositories also include Rust and TypeScript.

I am useful early, when requirements still arrive as conversation. I write down the flows, find the missing decisions, and agree on what can actually be checked before I settle on the implementation.

02How I use AI agents

I use agents to read unfamiliar code, compare options, and draft bounded changes. They save a lot of time, but a plausible answer is not the same thing as a checked one.

Agents inspect and implement. I decide what the requirement means, where the boundary goes, and what counts as verified. Generated code remains a draft until I have read it in context and run the path that can fail.

On money paths and anything irreversible I do not accept generated code until I have traced the retry and failure branches myself.

03How I keep myself honest

I measure before changing a system when the result can be measured. I also try a deliberately wrong input: if the API, test, or counter accepts that too, the first green result did not prove much.

Changes get a second reading after the fix, and my reports separate what I checked from what I did not. The examples, including several times this caught my own mistake, are collected in How I check my own work.

04Why this site is built like a library

The site keeps the decision, the rejected option, the verification, and the limit beside the result. That gives a reader something more useful than a stack list and gives me fewer places to hide a convenient conclusion.

If a fact is not ready to publish, I leave a visible marker. I would rather come back to a blank than fill it with a number I cannot trace.

05What I am looking for

Backend and applied AI work where the problem is not yet well posed and the result has to be dependable. Remote or on site.

The site is available in English and Russian. GitHub is my public contact route and the best place to see what I am building now.
