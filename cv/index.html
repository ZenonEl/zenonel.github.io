01Profile

Around two years of commercial backend development, mostly in Python. I take tasks with unclear requirements and carry them through to a release. When an unfamiliar stack is required I learn how it is built, lean on earlier experience, and bring AI agents into analysis and implementation. AI speeds the work up. Responsibility for the decisions and the quality stays with me.

I choose architecture by the scale and the risk of the task and check critical scenarios by hand as well as with tests. Before production, changes go through subagent review until LGTM.

04Projects

A workflow for agent-assisted work: mnemo keeps raw context and provenance, herald is the boundary between agents and communication channels, ephemeris carries the state of the day between sessions as source addresses rather than a retelling, and kanon records before the work what the result will be accepted on.
