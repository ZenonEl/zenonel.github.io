01Profile

Around two years of commercial backend development, mostly in Python. I take tasks with unclear requirements and carry them through to a release. When an unfamiliar stack is required I learn how it is built, lean on earlier experience, and bring AI agents into analysis and implementation. AI speeds the work up. Responsibility for the decisions and the quality stays with me.

I choose architecture by the scale and the risk of the task, check critical scenarios by hand as well as with tests, version releases, gate changes in CI, and keep work moving through issues and small pull requests.
